The House of Zilbershlag

A name entrusted with fine things for three generations.

What began in 1962 as one collector's pursuit of the rare and the beautiful has become a private institution — a house known for discretion, scholarship and an unerring eye. Each object is researched, documented and catalogued with the care of an archive.

Today the collection opens its doors. Whether by daily auction, the weekly signature sale, or quiet private acquisition, every piece carries the provenance and assurance of the House.
